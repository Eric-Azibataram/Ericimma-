About Me

The person behind the pixels.

Hi, I'm Eric Azibataram

I'm a web designer and digital practitioner from Otuogidi community, Bayelsa State, Nigeria. I design websites, create graphics, and edit and animate videos, and I enjoy teaching others how to do the same.

I graduated in Microbiology from the Federal University Otuoke, and along the way I trained in graphic design, video animation and video editing, with certificates including one from Denitech. Today I combine those skills to help people and businesses show up well online.

I also run the YouTube channels Learn Tech with Eric and Eric naija gist, and publish technical write-ups on dev.to.
