Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: November 2026. This website is run by Eric Azibataram. This page explains what information is collected when you use it.

Information you give me

If you book a session, I collect your name, email address, optional phone or WhatsApp number, the service you want, and the message you write. If you subscribe, I collect your email address. I use this only to reply to you, deliver what you asked for, and send the updates you signed up for. You can ask me to delete your details at any time.

Payments

Paid lessons are paid for through Paystack. Your card or bank details are entered on Paystack’s secure page and are never stored on this website. I only keep your email address, the lesson you bought and the amount.

Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can control personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

The website also uses a small cookie to keep the site owner logged in to the private dashboard. It is not used to track visitors.

Sharing your information

I do not sell your information. It is shared only with the services needed to run the website: the hosting provider, the database and file storage provider, the email sending service, and Paystack for payments.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from updates at any time. Use the booking page to contact me.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed.
